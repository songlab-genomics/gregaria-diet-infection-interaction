fat body RNA-seq / diet / fungal challenge

Fat body diet-infection RNA-seq analysis

This workflowR site is organized as a collaborator-facing analysis trail: start with the fat body experiment and DESeq2 contrasts, then move to candidate genes, enrichment, and reference-context overlays that ask whether experiment DEGs also resemble tissue or phase signatures.

How To Read This Site

The website now has one recommended path rather than a flat list of every page. Use Fat Body Experiment for the primary DESeq2 results and sample-filter/outlier sensitivity scenarios. Use Interpretation for candidate genes, GO/KEGG, and publication panels. Use Reference Context only after the experiment DEGs have been defined, to ask whether fat body DEGs also overlap tissue or phase signatures. Older exploratory pages remain under Archive for provenance, but they are no longer the main route through the analysis.

Recommended Reading Path

Step 1 Study Design and Data

Confirm the diet, infection, sample metadata, rRNA-filtered counts, and inclusion logic.

Step 2 RNA-seq Workflow

Document how reads move from trimming, STAR alignment, and featureCounts into the count matrix.

Step 3 Fat Body Count QC

Check library sizes, PCA structure, sample distances, and broad expression quality.

Step 4 Fat Body Infection DEGs

Define infected-versus-control DEGs within each diet using all fat body samples.

Step 5 Diet x Infection Model

Check the formal full-model terms for diet, treatment, and their interaction.

Step 6 Outlier-Removed DEGs

Repeat the key fat body DEG views after removing the current outlier set.

Step 7 Candidate Genes

Focus on insect-relevant immune, energy-store, amino-acid, nutrition, and stress genes.

Step 8 GO and KEGG Enrichment

Review eggNOG-derived enrichment after understanding the DEG foregrounds.

Step 9 Term-to-Gene Index

Search which DEG IDs contributed to each enriched GO term or KEGG pathway.

Step 10 Reference Context

Ask whether fat body DEGs are shared with tissue-reference, head-phase, or thorax-phase signatures.

Step 11 Publication Figure Exports

Find rendered panels that can be assembled or refined for manuscripts and collaborator slides.

fat body RNA-seq / diet / fungal challenge

Fat body diet-infection RNA-seq analysis

Follow how dietary protein-to-carbohydrate balance relates to the fat body transcriptional response of Schistocerca gregaria to Metarhizium robertsii. The site moves from experimental design and quality control to differential expression, functional interpretation, and publication figures.

Start with the study Read the results View the figures

Follow the analysis

1 Design and quality control Understand the diets, infection treatment, sample cohort, mapping, and count filtering. 2 Primary differential expression Follow the manuscript analysis and the placement sensitivity that defines its gene universe. 3 Functional interpretation Connect infection-responsive genes to GO terms, KEGG pathways, expression clusters, and candidates. 4 Publication figures Review and download the main and supplementary figure panels with their source tables.

Primary analysis. The manuscript path uses the 44 QC-passed libraries, transcript-plus-exon gene counts, annotated rRNA removal, and genes assigned to the nuclear chromosomes. All-scaffold, exon-only, sample-exclusion, and sequence-origin checks are grouped under Validation.

Every analysis page contains its runnable R code. Data locations and rerun commands are documented on the workflow and reproducibility page.
